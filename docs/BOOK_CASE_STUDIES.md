# First two book case studies

These are **curated demonstrations** based on inspection of the supplied scanned pages, not a complete machine extraction of either book. PDF page numbers refer to the printed page shown in the scan.

## Hartshorne — Algebraic Geometry (Russian 1981)
- Chapter I §1, printed p.17: affine algebraic sets as common zeros of polynomials.
- Chapter I §2, printed pp.30–32: projective varieties, cones, intersections of quadrics.
- Proposed visual experiments: circle as a zero set, intersecting quadrics, switching affine/projective viewpoint.
- Important: a real algebraic variety is not exhausted by its real-coordinate rendering. Complex points, scheme structure, multiplicity and topology must not be silently conflated.

## Sveshnikov, Bogolyubov, Kravtsov — Lectures on Mathematical Physics (1993)
- Chapter I §1, printed pp.9–15: longitudinal and transverse oscillations; wave equation (1.8).
- Chapter I §2, printed pp.25–27: heat conduction and boundary conditions.
- Proposed visual experiments: standing wave with fixed endpoints and diffusing sinusoidal temperature profile.
- These are idealized solutions with explicitly chosen parameters and boundary conditions, not reconstructed measurements.

## Cross-domain idea
Polynomial zeros describe the spatial geometry of constraints. PDEs describe families of functions evolving with time. A meaningful bridge is to visualize the zero level set of u(x,t) as time changes; this is a mathematical transformation, not evidence that the two books describe the same physical phenomenon.
